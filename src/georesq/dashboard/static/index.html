How to Connect Your Trained `v0.2` Checkpoint (`51 MB` fp16) Locally

To keep Git history lightweight (<15 MB), `.pth` model weights and `.tif` GeoTIFFs are excluded via .gitignore. Place your Kaggle-exported lite_gated_fusion_v0_2_fp16.pth (51 MB) and optional demo_unseen_tiles.zip (3 MB) inside exports/: 

cd /Users/rakib/GeoResQ/GeoResQ
mkdir -p exports
# Move downloaded Kaggle exports into exports/ (auto-detected by dashboard!):
mv ~/Downloads/lite_gated_fusion_v0_2_fp16.pth exports/ 2>/dev/null || true
unzip -o ~/Downloads/demo_unseen_tiles.zip -d exports/ 2>/dev/null || true

# Launch interactive visual dashboard on Apple Silicon MPS / CUDA / CPU:
python3 scripts/run_dashboard.py --port 8000

Once placed in exports/, refresh this page: the top-right pill turns green (Trained PyTorch Checkpoint on MPS/CUDA) and real marshall-wildfire & noto-earthquake GeoTIFF tiles appear automatically in the Scene dropdown!

Responsible-AI Guardrails & Physical Limitations
  • Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Verification: Ranked sectors (R1C1..R4C4) and Shannon entropy maps are triage aids for qualified imagery analysts, never autonomous dispatch triggers.
  • Sub-Roof Damage Ambiguity (Class 2): Floodwater silt (libya-flood) and shockwave facade damage (beirut-explosion) preserve nadir roof silhouettes, routing moderate damage to Intact.
  • Pancaked Timber Rubble vs. Ash/Lava: Multimodal SAR fusion excels when corner reflections vanish (lava/wildfire ash), whereas fractured ceramic roof rubble (noto-earthquake) retains strong radar backscatter.
  • Prohibited Contexts: Strictly prohibited for armed-conflict targeting, surveillance, or automated insurance/financial claim denial.
